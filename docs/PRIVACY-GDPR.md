# Privacy notice — operator template

This is a starting point, not legal advice or a compliance certification. Operators must document their own purposes, retention, destinations, and legal basis.

Depending on enabled modules, Overseer may process Steam IDs/display names, playtime/session records, moderation notes/reports, roles, reward claims, grid/limit data, and staff audit events. Configured webhook routes send selected events to operator-selected destinations. Bifrost stores salted password hashes and bounded in-memory sessions.

Never store custom Discord status text or message bodies. Presence roster access must stay behind Analytics authorization. Label approximate counts when Presence and Server Members intents are unavailable.

Operators must secure backups/credentials, disclose configured processing, restrict staff access, and honor applicable requests. Replace this template with server-specific retention/contact details.
